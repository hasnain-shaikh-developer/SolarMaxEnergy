FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about solar energy and our services in Pakistan.

The cost varies based on system size and location. Residential systems in Pakistan typically range from ₨450,000 to ₨1,800,000 before net metering benefits. With government incentives and net metering, your payback period can be as short as 3-5 years.

Most residential installations are completed within 1-3 days. However, the entire process from consultation to net metering activation typically takes 4-8 weeks, including NEPRA approvals and DISCO interconnection.

Standard grid-tied systems shut down during outages for safety. However, with our battery backup and hybrid inverter solutions, you can keep essential appliances running during load shedding. We recommend adding battery storage for complete energy independence.

Yes! Solar panels generate electricity even on cloudy days, typically producing 10-25% of their rated capacity. Pakistan receives abundant sunshine year-round, making it an ideal location for solar energy.

Solar panels require minimal maintenance. Occasional cleaning to remove dust and debris, plus annual inspections, is usually sufficient. Our maintenance packages include monitoring, cleaning, and performance optimization.

Most roofs are suitable for solar. Ideal conditions include south-facing orientation, minimal shading, and a roof in good condition. Our experts conduct a free site assessment to determine your roof's solar potential.
